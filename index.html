About

Intelligent systems, from theory to impact

Dr. Hedieh Sajedi is an Associate Professor of Artificial Intelligence at the University of Tehran. She received her B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Amirkabir University of Technology, and her M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence) from Sharif University of Technology. Her work spans machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and information security, with applications ranging from medical and biological data to image forensics.

Research

Areas of expertise

A research programme connecting rigorous AI methods with real-world data.

🧠

Machine & Deep Learning

Neural architectures, recurrent and convolutional models, and learning from limited or noisy data.

👁️

Computer Vision

Pattern recognition and image analysis, including medical and scientific imaging.

🔐

Steganography & Steganalysis

Data hiding, watermarking and detection techniques, a long-standing line of her research.

🧬

AI for Life Sciences

Machine learning for biological and bioinformatics problems, in collaboration with biologists.

🛡️

Image Encryption & Forensics

Chaos-based encryption and digital media forensic detection.

📈

Time-Series & Forecasting

Sequence models, such as bidirectional RNNs, for prediction in climate and other domains.
